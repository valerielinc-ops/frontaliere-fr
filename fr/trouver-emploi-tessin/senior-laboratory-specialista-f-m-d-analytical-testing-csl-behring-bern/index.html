For our global Technical Operations Organization, we are currently looking for a

Senior Laboratory Specialist – Analytical Testing (m/f/x)

Fullltime / Permanent / CF Level 04 Locations available: Bern

The Opportunity Researches and develops innovative medicines to address unmet medical needs, from early discovery through clinical trials to commercialization. It ensures that all products meet the highest clinical safety standards and comply with global regulatory requirements. The role involves analyzing complex data, managing research processes, and maintaining technical documentation and systems. It also includes designing and implementing experimental and operational processes to support efficient product development and informed decision-making. In addition, the position leads projects and cross-functional teams, overseeing portfolio prioritization, resource allocation, and risk management. Finally, it contributes to the development and optimization of manufacturing processes to enable efficient, scalable, and compliant production.

Main Responsibilities and Accountabilities

  • Supports the team in leveraging technology platform capabilities to contribute to project goals.​
  • Assists in the coordination of local and global research and development activities under guidance.​
  • Participates in collaborative efforts with other CSL sites and external partners, with supervision.​
  • Applies foundational knowledge of relevant scientific disciplines to support laboratory tasks and project execution.​
  • Works under close to moderate supervision to meet operational objectives with limited impact on broader departmental outcomes.​
