Quel est le salaire net avec CHF 95'000 brut en tant que frontalier?

Un nouveau frontalier avec CHF 95'000 brut perçoit environ CHF 55'293 net par an (CHF 4'608/mois).

Quel barème fiscal s'applique pour CHF 95'000 marié?

Comment le salaire net est calculé

With a gross annual income of CHF 95'000, the Swiss employer withholds mandatory social contributions of approximately CHF 12'255: AVS (5.3%), unemployment insurance (1.1%), accident insurance (0.7%), daily sickness allowance (0.8%), and occupational pension LPP. The Ticino withholding tax is calculated using table tableB, resulting in an effective rate of 7.8%.

Régime fiscal appliqué

As a new cross-border worker (2024+ agreement), income is subject to concurrent taxation: 100% of the withholding tax stays in Switzerland. Italian IRPEF applies with a €10,000 deduction and proportional tax credit for Swiss taxes paid.

Impact de la situation familiale

As a married person, tax table B applies with reduced rates. Swiss family allowances amount to CHF 0/year.

Zone de distance frontalière

Living over 20 km from the border, 100% of the withholding tax stays in Switzerland. Italian IRPEF applies in full with proportional credit.

Budget mensuel indicatif

Monthly, a Swiss resident nets approximately CHF 5'578, while an Italian cross-border worker receives about EUR 5.050/month (at CHF/EUR 1.096).
