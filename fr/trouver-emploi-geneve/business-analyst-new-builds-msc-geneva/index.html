Your Purpose

The New Builds Business Analyst plays a key role in ensuring the financial, commercial, and operational success of MSC Cruises' new ship development projects.

Acting as the financial and analytical partner to the New Builds department, this role is responsible for defining, monitoring, and optimizing departmental budgets, overseeing owner-supplied materials (OS&E and FF&E where applicable), and ensuring sound financial governance throughout the project lifecycle.
